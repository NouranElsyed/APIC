Nesting result

Double-click a part to pick it up: it follows the mouse (move it onto another sheet of the same thickness to transfer it), scroll the wheel to rotate, click to place, Esc to cancel. It can't overlap other parts, break the spacing, or enter the margin (it stays at the last allowed position).
Import parts, then press Optimize.
